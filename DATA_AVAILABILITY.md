# Data and artifact availability

Tracked run JSON/SVG files are manufactured numerical or simulated-measurement artifacts. They do not contain admitted paired human FORCE/LPT records and do not establish instrument calibration, population norms, human biomechanics or clinical/athlete validity. Historical source snapshots are software regression fixtures.

The current source/fixture inventory is `runs/i3/res503/provenance.json`, frozen to its recorded Git revision. Later issue receipts identify their exact source/command/result provenance. Existing I0/I1/I2 outputs remain available locally without silently regenerating or replacing historical negative evidence.

No external participant data, vendor corpus, historical holdout or empirical reference is distributed by the I3 code release. Ignored research and sibling evidence are excluded. No public data access or redistribution permission follows from selecting a software license. The registry identity inventory has a separate rights-confirmation requirement.

Owned synthetic fixtures and figures are proposed for the same license as owned code/docs, subject to owner confirmation. No license grant currently applies to this repository. Record the exact reviewed software revision, parameter hypotheses, clock/stage assumptions, refusal status and claim ceilings whenever reusing an artifact after licensing.
