# RES-493 back squat: continuous-support multi-repetition dynamics

_Eccentric, reversal, concentric and lockout repetitions of a barbell back squat, generated by forward dynamics with both feet on separate plates throughout._

---

## 🏋️ Topology and protocol

The model is the RES-492 bilateral human with a barbell on the posterior shoulder shelf, see [squat.py](../../biosyngen/i2/squat.py).

**Bar support.** The bar rests on a unilateral frictional contact (μ = 0.8 declared). Slippage is therefore possible: it is detected and refused, never prevented by a weld.

**Feet.** Both feet stay on their plates. A foot leaving its plate is refused as `FLIGHT_FORBIDDEN`.

**Safety pins** are an optional frictionless world contact under the bar. Any load on the pins makes that repetition `SUPPORT_INCOMPLETE`, and its metrics are refused.

**Protocol.** SquatProtocol declares load, repetitions, stance and bottom postures, descent and ascent tempo, bottom pause, lockout hold, bar friction, pin height and ascent effort. Each repetition is a staged control hypothesis:

1. minimum-jerk descent;
2. optional pause;
3. minimum-jerk ascent with saturating joint PD (Hill torque–velocity limits apply);
4. lockout hold.

**Setup refusals:**
- a posture that cannot be held statically → `UNSAFE_OR_INFEASIBLE_TORQUE_DEMAND`;
- a shelf friction that cannot hold the bar statically → `BAR_SLIPPAGE_AT_SETUP`.

## 📏 Latent repetition events (bar and COM mechanics only)

| Event | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Descent onset | Bar vertical velocity < −0.02 m/s |
| Bottom reversal | Bar velocity crosses 0 upward |
| Ascent onset | Bar velocity > +0.05 m/s (above the end-of-descent rebound) |
| Rep end | \|v\| ≤ 0.02 m/s with the bar back near its start height |
| Sticking region | Local velocity maximum > 0.1 m/s, then a dip ≥ 5 % of the peak ascent velocity, then re-acceleration by the same amount |
| Minimum repetition | 0.05 m of bar descent |

All thresholds are declared numerical choices, not device thresholds. Work is reported as three separate quantities: joint (actuator + passive) work, work on the bar by the shoulders, and work by the foot forces on the system COM.

## 🚧 Limitations and claim ceiling

- **No arms or hands.** The bar rests on a point contact with no grip force. A real lifter's hands add a stabilizing grip, so the slippage controls are conservative.
- **Planar model.** There is no knee valgus, no frontal-plane balance and no spine model; the HAT is one rigid segment.
- **Control is declared.** Tempo is imposed by minimum-jerk references. The sticking region at 160 kg comes from capacity and torque–velocity limits under that strategy. It is not a measured human sticking region.
- **Heavy loads.** At 160 kg the toes release briefly near lockout (heels carry the load). This is reported in the events and not hidden.
- **Uncalibrated values.** All values are uncalibrated. No empirical squat data was compared: NOT_EVALUABLE.
