# RES-492 vertical jump family on the bilateral multibody human

_CMJ, SJ, loaded CMJ, loaded jump squat and landing, generated by forward dynamics on the RES-491 engine._

---

## 🧭 What is implemented

| Subtype | Load topology | Start state | Distinct physics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CMJ | None | Exact static quiet stance | Countermovement (unweighting, braking), propulsion, flight, landing |
| SJ | None | Exact static squat hold | Concentric-only propulsion; a countermovement would be refused (`SJ_COUNTERMOVEMENT_PRESENT`) |
| LOADED_CMJ | 20 kg vest welded to the trunk (LOAD_LINK) | Load-balanced quiet stance | Heavier system; vest moves rigidly with the trunk |
| LOADED_JUMP_SQUAT | 30 kg bar on the posterior shoulder shelf (unilateral frictional contact) | Load-balanced quiet stance | Shallower countermovement; bar separates in late propulsion and re-impacts the shoulders |
| DROP_JUMP, REPEATED_REBOUND_JUMP | — | — | Refused (`NOT_IMPLEMENTED`): rigid point-contact feet have no qualified compliance |

- [human.py](../../biosyngen/i2/human.py): body, loads and anthropometry;
- [simulate.py](../../biosyngen/i2/simulate.py): recorder and declared control;
- [jumps.py](../../biosyngen/i2/jumps.py): scenarios;
- [phases.py](../../biosyngen/i2/phases.py): latent events;
- [instruments.py](../../biosyngen/i2/instruments.py): FORCE and LPT.

## 🦴 Body, contacts and actuation

The model has seven rigid segments: a HAT segment plus a left and right thigh, shank and foot. Segment fractions are Winter values (recalled, not inspected), split per leg. Each foot has heel and metatarsal contact points that form one friction patch, and each foot binds to its own plate frame (FZ_LEFT / FZ_RIGHT).

Each joint has an extensor and a flexor actuator. Each actuator has activation dynamics and a Hill torque–velocity relation. Capacities are half of the I1 lumped values. All of these are ASSUMED_UNCALIBRATED.

Control is a staged joint PD with a static gravity-compensation feedforward and an ankle balance term referenced to the mean ankle position. Stages switch on:

- declared command times;
- proprioception (knee rate);
- touch (foot contact modes).

Stage switches are reported as LATENT_CONTROL_HYPOTHESIS. Movement events are never read from the controller. How the strategies were chosen is recorded in [strategy_search.md](../../runs/i2/res492/strategy_search.md).

## 📏 Latent events and phases

[phases.py](../../biosyngen/i2/phases.py) derives events from the summed foot force, the COM state and the foot contact modes:

| Event | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Kinetic onset | First interval with \|Fz − W\| > 10⁻⁶ W |
| Peak downward velocity | Fz returns to W from below |
| COM velocity reversal | COM vertical velocity crosses 0 upward |
| Left/right foot release | Both points of that foot open |
| Takeoff | The later foot release |
| Touchdown | First foot impact after takeoff |
| Left/right foot contact | First impact of that foot after takeoff |
| Landing lowest point | COM vertical velocity crosses 0 upward after touchdown |

The CMJ phases are QUIET_STANCE, UNWEIGHTING, BRAKING, PROPULSION, FLIGHT, LANDING and POST_LANDING. SJ has STATIC_HOLD and PROPULSION, then flight and landing; it has no UNWEIGHTING or BRAKING.

## 📡 Simulated instruments

**FORCE_PIPE_01** reads the per-plate contact forces. Interval-average forces are applied as a zero-order hold, and impacts as Dirac impulses. The signal then passes through:

1. a per-plate gain/cross-talk/offset stage;
2. a 250 Hz Butterworth filter;
3. noise and a 16-bit ADC with saturation flags;
4. a shared plate clock with offset, drift and jitter.

FZ_TOTAL = FZ_LEFT + FZ_RIGHT is a source-preserving foot-contact sum. It is not the net force on athlete plus load.

**LPT_PIPE_01** measures cable length from a floor anchor to a hip belt, or to the bar collar for the back-bar jump squat. It models reel slack and encoder quantization and runs on its own clock. Provider velocity is derived from the exported displacement.

## 🚧 Limitations and claim ceiling

- **Rigid contact.** Feet are rigid with point contacts, so landing loading includes impulses (heel strike 19–31 N·s). Landing peak force is NOT_EVALUABLE; only impulses and post-impact forces are mechanically meaningful. No compliant heel pad, tissue or shoe model exists, and drop and rebound jumps are refused for that reason.
- **Planar model.** Left and right legs share one sagittal plane. There is no frontal-plane balance, no arms and no arm swing.
- **Control is a declared strategy.** It is not neural truth. It was tuned for mechanical plausibility (near-vertical takeoff, recoverable landing). The SJ ≈ CMJ takeoff similarity is not the usual human result, because there is no series elasticity.
- **Uncalibrated values.** Anthropometry, capacities, gains and device stages are uncalibrated. Takeoff velocities of 1.4–1.9 m/s are synthetic outcomes, not predictions for a person.
- **Instrument clocks.** LPT and plate clocks are independent. Synchronization is not established by sample alignment.

Claim ceilings: NUMERICAL_VERIFICATION_ONLY, SIMULATED_MEASUREMENT_ONLY, BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION=NOT_ESTABLISHED, REAL_PAIRED_FORCE_LPT_VALIDATION=NOT_ESTABLISHED.
