The small curated CMJ grid (67.5/75/82.5 kg × symmetric capacity scale 0.9/1.0/1.1) completes 9/9 model tasks. This is a deterministic authored grid, not an independent population sample or evidence of subject calibration.

Results, errors and full available traces are retained in `controller-stress/`; no failing case was optimized away. The ordinary Olympic-technique claim remains unqualified.

CMJ and SJ nominal system takeoff velocities are 1.9112748167399893 and 1.886924197907734 m/s. They have different initial posture, activation initialization and staged command histories; all initial activation states are recorded. This observation cannot identify one causal mechanism. The inspected [Bobbert et al. primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8933491/) attributes the advantage in its particular study/model to active-state/force buildup before shortening, rather than elastic reuse. That supports investigating activation/coordination/initial-state effects; it does not validate this joint-torque surrogate or establish a universal explanation. No article/data payload or participant record was admitted as a benchmark reference.

## Remaining numerical and scientific boundaries

* First-order symplectic stepping, within-step capture, finite event localization and position/velocity projection have discretization error. `contact_work_estimate_j`, passive/active joint quadrature and the vertical bar component are not exact integrated mechanical/metabolic work. COM pseudo-work is not stationary-ground-contact work.
* Body-attached plane transport is independently challenged in the specified analytical control; arbitrary world-normal internal contacts, all coupled impact configurations, rotated instrument frames and all parameter/control regimes are not exhaustively qualified.
* Modeled event timestamps and sampling brackets are numerical conventions, not mathematical integration-error bounds or empirical event-timing uncertainty.
* Front-rack catch/turnover, overhead receipt, full clean/snatch, hang/block starts, compliant drop/rebound/landing loading, 3-D wrist/scapula/foot relocation and empirical bilateral/paired measurement remain NOT_IMPLEMENTED/NOT_EVALUABLE as appropriate. No original refusal is removed.
* Future fidelity work is separately scoped: actual hand/bar stabilization; articulated receipt geometry; compliant contact; robust control/parameter identification; independently rights-cleared metrology and human reference acquisition. None is silently delivered by this release.
