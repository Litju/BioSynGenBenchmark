## Execute, diagnose and continue

1. Implement actual issue requirements in coherent, independently testable gates. Reproduce relevant RED witnesses before repair, develop a specific physical or software hypothesis, run focused positives and falsifiers, inspect results, commit verified improvements and continue to the next eligible gate. A plan, source inventory, passing CI badge, running monitor or draft PR is not the end of an implementation task.
2. **Two unsuccessful repairs of the same failure require a diagnostic pivot, not automatic termination of the issue or campaign.** Stop repeating the same attempted mechanism or retuning parameters. Preserve the original RED evidence; inspect contact/force, support, geometry, controller, timing, energy and numerical logs as appropriate; formulate a materially new falsifiable root-cause hypothesis or alternate modeling strategy and test it with bounded resources. If no defensible next repair exists, leave that specific scientific claim `FAIL` or `NOT_EVALUABLE` and proceed with other authorized independent gates. Do not fabricate extra attempts, change budgets just to pass, or enter an unbounded tuning loop.
3. **Six elapsed hours is a checkpoint, not an automatic stop.** Preserve clean commits or exact worktree status, record running jobs, results, limits and the next executable step; continue eligible work when execution capacity and authorization permit. Observe actual environment limits and explicit user stop requests. Never promise to continue or deliver later unless a real scheduled automation was created.
4. **A blocker applies to the affected path, not every unrelated task.** Record the failing command, SHA, independent reference, expected/observed outcome and missing prerequisite. Continue independent permitted work. If scientific qualification cannot pass, retain `FAIL`/`REFUSED`/`NOT_IMPLEMENTED`/`EVIDENCE_PENDING` with correct denominators; do not relabel missing mechanics, data or scientific acceptance as complete.
5. **Hard stops remain mandatory** for actual safety/security/rights breaches, leaked secrets, unapproved destructive operations or irreversible consumption, missing essential owner authority, repository corruption, or an explicit current owner stop. Isolate and report the scope; never bypass a scientific gate, protected data boundary or externally required approval to satisfy a completion target. A repeated failing physical test is not itself authorization to merge or declare success.

This current owner-authorized continuation policy **supersedes the old repository-wide two-repair / six-hour hard-stop sentence** for future execution. It does not erase historical failures or override separately explicit, currently binding safety, experimental, holdout or owner-only approvals.

## Scientific credibility and independent evidence

- Maintain independent evaluator and source roles. Synthetic model/TRUTH is not real sensor truth. FORCE and LPT have separate acquisition clocks, geometry and observability; do not conflate attachment/cable length, COM motion, bar motion and anatomical events.
- Preserve original numerical positives, falsifying controls, failed traces, frozen test partitions and reproducible receipts. Qualify any new plant/control topology on its **own** final revision; legacy V1 successes do not certify V2 dynamics. Apply numerical tolerance only when justified by independent analytical reference, convergence or measurement uncertainty.
- Distinguish implementer verification from independent final-SHA scientific review. Passing unit tests, positive optimizer exit, pose reachability, plausible rendered motion or a green hosted run never substitutes for physical contact/energy/actuation checks or external biological validation.
- Maintain claim ceilings: `NUMERICAL_VERIFICATION_ONLY`; `SIMULATED_MEASUREMENT_ONLY`; `BIOLOGICAL_VALIDATION=NOT_ESTABLISHED`; `REAL_PAIRED_FORCE_LPT_VALIDATION=NOT_ESTABLISHED`. A known failed overhead grasp or incomplete drop recovery must remain visibly unqualified.
